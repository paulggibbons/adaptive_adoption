# Behavioral Governance™ — The Guardrails

![Behavioral Governance™ framework](../visuals-and-presentations/behavioral-governance/canonical-framework.png)

> Synchronized from the [canonical website](https://paulgibbonsadvisory.com/behavioral-governance/) on 2026-09-06.

The fatal mistake in all governance frameworks is that they are anti-change frameworks, not change frameworks. Policies and policing. Brakes not accelerators.

Design governance to “go hard and go safely.” Design for behaviors, not pdfs.
