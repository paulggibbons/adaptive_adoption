# Adaptive Adoption™

**The human side of AI adoption: Change Agility™, Leadership Delta™, and Behavioral Governance™.**

Adaptive Adoption brings together the capabilities organisations build, the behaviours leaders practise, and the governance that enables responsible action. Three disciplines, 20 pillars and dimensions, with practical tools and an explicit intellectual lineage.

## Three disciplines

| Discipline | Focus | Structure |
